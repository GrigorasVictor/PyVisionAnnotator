Abordările hibride pentru object detection și image segmentation combină localizarea obiectelor cu segmentarea precisă la nivel de pixel într-un pipeline unificat, îmbunătățind atât acuratețea detecției, cât și înțelegerea semantică a imaginii.

Cascade R-CNN: Delving into High Quality Object Detection is a 2019 computer vision research paper and book by Zhaowei Cai and Nuno Vasconcelos. It presents a novel multi-stage framework for object detection that improves localization accuracy and robustness across varying intersection-over-union (IoU) thresholds. The work has become foundational in advancing deep learning–based detection systems.

Key facts
Authors: Zhaowei Cai and Nuno Vasconcelos

Published: 2019 (IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition)

Field: Computer vision, object detection

Core concept: Multi-stage, cascaded refinement of bounding box classifiers

Influence: Widely adopted in research and industrial detection pipelines

Background and Motivation
Prior object detectors such as Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN often underperformed at high IoU thresholds, indicating imprecise bounding boxes. Cascade R-CNN addressed this limitation by creating a sequence of detection stages, each trained with a progressively higher IoU target, enabling the model to refine predictions iteratively and better align boxes with objects.

Mechanism and Architecture
The architecture extends the two-stage R-CNN paradigm by cascading multiple detection heads. Each stage receives bounding boxes refined from the previous stage and is trained to handle a higher localization quality. This design mitigates overfitting to low-IoU samples and naturally balances positive–negative sample distributions, resulting in more accurate detections without additional complex tuning.
