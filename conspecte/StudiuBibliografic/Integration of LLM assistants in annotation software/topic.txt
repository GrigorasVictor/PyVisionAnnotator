Integrarea unui asistent bazat pe LLM într-o aplicație software permite oferirea de suport conversațional inteligent, facilitând interacțiunea utilizatorului cu sistemul prin răspunsuri contextuale și automatizarea task-urilor de asistență.

LLaVA
LLaVA (Large Language and Vision Assistant) is an open-source multimodal AI model that connects a large language model with a visual encoder to enable image-grounded dialogue and reasoning. Introduced in 2023, it represents one of the earliest end-to-end trained large multimodal models capable of GPT-4-like visual question answering and conversational understanding.

Key facts
Full name: Large Language and Vision Assistant

First paper: “Visual Instruction Tuning” (arXiv:2304.08485, 2023)

Creators: Haotian Liu, Chunyuan Li, Qingyang Wu, Yong Jae Lee

Base language model: Vicuna (fine-tuned LLaMA)

Initial benchmark: 92.5 % accuracy on Science QA

Origin and concept
LLaVA was introduced in the paper Visual Instruction Tuning as a proof-of-concept for combining language-only instruction tuning with visual understanding. The authors used GPT-4 to generate multimodal instruction–response pairs and fine-tuned a vision–language system on this synthetic dataset. The resulting model learned to follow natural-language instructions about images, achieving strong zero-shot generalization on unseen visual tasks.

LLaVA integrates a pretrained vision encoder—typically the CLIP ViT-L/14 model—with a large language model such as Vicuna. A small projection module maps visual embeddings into the language model’s token space, enabling joint attention over textual and visual inputs. This end-to-end training contrasts with prior stage-wise or modular multimodal approaches.

Capabilities and impact
LLaVA can hold multimodal conversations, answer image-based questions, describe scenes, and explain visual reasoning steps. It approximates the conversational quality of multimodal GPT-4 while remaining fully open source and computationally efficient. Its release catalyzed a wave of successor models—LLaVA-1.5, LLaVA-Next, and specialized variants such as LLaVA-Med, MoE-LLaVA, and iLLaVA—extending the framework to medicine, efficiency optimization, and fine-grained reasoning.

Research significance
LLaVA marked a shift toward accessible multimodal assistants that unify perception and reasoning in one trainable pipeline. Its methodology for “visual instruction tuning” remains a foundational technique for developing open multimodal large language models and continues to influence academic and industrial research in general-purpose AI assistants.
