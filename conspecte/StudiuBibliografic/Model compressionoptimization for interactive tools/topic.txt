Model compression și optimization reprezintă tehnici utilizate pentru reducerea dimensiunii și complexității modelelor de inteligență artificială, permițând inferență rapidă și eficientă în aplicații interactive și dispozitive cu resurse limitate.



DistilBERT is a 2019 research paper and model introducing a compact, faster version of the large language model BERT. Developed by Hugging Face, it applies knowledge distillation to reduce BERT’s size and computational cost while retaining most of its performance on key natural language understanding benchmarks.

Key facts
Authors: Victor Sanh, Lysandre Debut, Julien Chaumond, Thomas Wolf

Published: 2019 (arXiv preprint)

Base model: BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers)

Compression ratio: ~40% smaller, ~60% faster inference

Performance: Retains ~97% of BERT’s accuracy on GLUE benchmark

Background and Motivation
BERT set a new standard for language understanding but required heavy computational resources, limiting deployment. DistilBERT was designed to make transformer models practical for production use on smaller hardware, such as CPUs and mobile devices, without a major sacrifice in accuracy. It distilled BERT’s knowledge into a leaner model with fewer parameters.

Knowledge Distillation Process
The distillation process trains a smaller “student” model to reproduce the behavior of a larger “teacher” model. DistilBERT uses BERT as the teacher, optimizing a combination of language modeling, distillation, and cosine embedding losses. It halves the number of layers (from 12 to 6) while maintaining key attention and embedding structures.

Impact and Adoption
DistilBERT became one of the first widely used efficient transformer models and a default lightweight option in the Hugging Face Transformers ecosystem. It spurred a line of subsequent model compression efforts and remains influential in the development of efficient transformer-based NLP systems for both research and real-world applications.
